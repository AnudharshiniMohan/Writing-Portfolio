About me

I'm a 2025 Computer Science Engineering graduate building my career in copywriting and content writing. My interest in writing comes from observing everyday experiences, understanding emotions, and finding meaningful ways to express them.

I enjoy writing personal reflections, poetry, advertising concepts, brand copy, and short scripts. I approach writing with curiosity, research, attention to detail, and an interest in understanding what makes an audience connect with a message.
